# Curated CS Search Seeds

This is the **initial high-signal seed set** for growing a quality-first CS corpus.

Selection principles:

- prefer canonical documentation, textbooks, course notes, and expert technical writing
- avoid broad UGC, low-signal aggregators, vendor marketing, and noisy community forums
- keep the initial crawl focused on sources that are likely to produce strong answers for real CS queries
- expand later only when retrieval quality improves

The list below is intentionally curated rather than exhaustive.
